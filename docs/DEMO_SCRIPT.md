# 5-minute demo video script (limit is 5:00)

Record with a real phone, screen mirrored or filmed, sound on. Have your blind/low-vision validator do the key steps themselves if they agree.

| Time | Show | Say (short) |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:25 | Validator holding a note | The problem in their own words (use a real quote, with permission). |
| 0:25-0:50 | Phone in airplane mode, app opens from home screen | Works offline, installs from a link, Hausa and English. |
| 0:50-2:00 | **Notes**: scan 3 different notes; speech + vibration | How it identifies notes; show "too dark" and "not sure" handling honestly. |
| 2:00-3:00 | **Alerts**: share a genuine-looking SMS, then a scam SMS from Messages | Normal alert still says check balance; scam gets warning + reasons. |
| 3:00-3:50 | **Transfer**: say amount and account; read-back; large amount asks twice | Catches an extra zero; it never moves money. |
| 3:50-4:20 | **Teach** tab + *Check my accuracy* | Measured held-out accuracy on real notes, weakest note, no photos stored. |
| 4:20-5:00 | Accessibility note highlights; what testers changed; pilot plan | One concrete change you made because of a tester. |

Tips: show one failure on camera and how the app handles it. Judges reward honesty, and "tested with a blind user" beats any feature.
